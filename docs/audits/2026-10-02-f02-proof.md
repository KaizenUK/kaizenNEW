Prepared 2 October 2026. Release verification is recorded below after deployment.

## Change

- Marketing calls to action use the Untitled UI primary, secondary and text-link styles. The three shared labels are "Talk to us about your site", "Check your website speed" and "See the case study". Ordinary navigation and form steps retain labels that describe their own action.
- Contact links now lead directly to the contact form. Landing-page links retain their existing on-page form target. Case links on the two landing pages still open a new tab, now with `noopener`.
- Forms, scanner controls, the speed-demo replay and the retained older marketing components use the same button component. Disabled and loading controls cannot activate by keyboard. Public buttons have at least 44px height and use DM Sans.
- The blog receives only the isolated button tokens, plugins and button font. Its full design remains B-01. Builder UI and Studio source are unchanged.
- Removed the temporary `/ui-test/` route and preview component. Removed the remaining unmeasured "30 seconds" promise beside the home speed-check link.
- Opening the existing mobile menu could crash when its Services promotion had no image. Reproduced on production at F-01, then guarded the optional media as the desktop menu already does. The complete navigation redesign remains F-06.
