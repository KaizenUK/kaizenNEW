# B-01: bring the guides into the main site design

Prepared 2 October 2026 from the task board, marketing skills, site profile and the B-02 screenshots. This is the remaining blog design task. B-02 owns the reviewed content, author identity and explicit checked dates.

## Reader and job

The index helps a business owner choose a guide about buying, checking or improving a website. A post should answer that question clearly, let the reader check its sources and author, and provide the existing next step. These are informational pages; they do not take the homepage's commercial web-design topic.

The diagnosed problems are visible in the existing screenshots: a different header and type system, a dark reading layout unrelated to the main site, and card rows stretched to the featured card's height. The phone index is 7,192px tall for nine guides. The blog also lacks a main landmark. These are observed design and accessibility issues, not measured causes of lost enquiries.

## Direction and boundaries

Use the already released F-02 to F-06 button, type, spacing, header and footer foundations. This does not require copying P-01's bespoke homepage hero, comparison or buyer-route patterns; Sean's review of those patterns remains pending under the board's suggested order.

Inspect the board's Untitled UI starting points before implementation: Featured post 01, Simple 01 and a blog-post page example. Choose a suitable reading and browsing layout for the actual nine guides. Avoid unnecessary search, filter or subscription controls. Real article images should have useful crops and reserved dimensions. Card height should follow content on phones, with no empty space added just to match another row.

For posts, keep a comfortable reading width, the main site's heading/body fonts, clear source links and visible keyboard focus. Put publication date, author and checked date together without making them look like the same date. Keep the article's answer and existing next step prominent. Preserve the exact agreed contact label and any existing reply line.

## Source scope

`LinearLayout.astro` serves only the blog index and article routes. It currently owns the dark stylesheet and separate `BlogNav`. `BlogIndex.astro` owns the oversized featured and regular cards; `BlogPost.astro` owns article framing and editor-link controls. `PortableTextRenderer.tsx` also has dark-specific image, quote, table, code and action styles that need inspection before a light variant is safe.

Preserve published CMS content, all nine URLs, metadata, canonical/indexability rules, explicit review records, source links, original publication timestamps, image proportions and the B-04 retirement. Keep editor-cookie behaviour and Studio links working. Do not change Studio schemas or the builder. Old components/styles can be removed only after checking all callers.
