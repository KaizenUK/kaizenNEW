## Result

No new dependency was added. See `client/components/untitled/blog/README.md` for provenance.

The obsolete separate blog navigation, featured-card motion component and dark stylesheet are removed after checking their callers. The legacy `LinearLayout.astro` filename remains for its two route callers. Guide-specific prose styles are scoped to `.blog-page`; builder and Studio source are unchanged. Quotes, tables, code, inline code, video captions and structured action blocks also work on the light background. Existing prose contact links remain descriptive text links.
