# B-04: one guide to fixing or replacing unfinished software

Prepared 2 October 2026 using the marketing messaging, page design and SEO skills, site profile and founder interview. This is a content merge and route retirement; B-01/B-02 still own blog design and author/date presentation.

## Reader and purpose

The reader owns a software project with missed dates or unclear progress. They know something is wrong but cannot yet judge whether to repair or replace it. The guide should help them request evidence and compare the remaining work before buying more work. The fear of paying again for another poor result comes from Sean's founder interview; its application to this article is an editorial hypothesis, not a measured reader finding.

The message order is: warning signs warrant a review, show what works, agree the smallest useful outcome, compare repair/replacement costs and risks, then set a checkable next decision. Preserve the retiring article's useful planning and ownership advice. Remove both articles' conflicting unsupported claims about the usual cause of failure and the proportion of recoverable projects. Do not promise that a rebuild or a planning reset will succeed.
