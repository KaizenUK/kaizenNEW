# P-04: show how Kaizen works through real work

Prepared 2 October 2026 during the P-03 deployment. Implemented against the verified P-03 baseline; local checks pass. Released in `5882cef`; deployment `36981405250` and live checks pass. Evidence: `docs/audits/2026-10-02-p04-proof.md`.

## Reader and job

An owner checking Kaizen after a referral or finding the service page wants to know who will look after the work. Their largest doubt is paying again and still being disappointed. Their next doubt is learning another system. The page should answer those concerns through Midland's work and the actual buying process, then make a free first conversation easy to start.

No photo of Sean. Do not describe staffing, add credentials, invent a quote or promise a free draft. Keep the approved same-person experience without implying a particular business size.

## Ranked messages and proof

1. You deal with the same person from first chat through launch and later changes. Identify business website work and put the agreed contact action, exact reply sentence and free first conversation in the first phone screen.
2. Show Midland's real before/after screenshots and explain the changes: clearer product choices and an oil finder. Place its approved result beside the work. Use the existing evidence record and check the actual assets before composing the section. The approved figures are more than eight seconds before, 1.2 seconds after and three times the enquiries in the first month. Several genuine enquiries a day is also approved; no universal outcome or ranking guarantee.
3. Explain the steps: first conversation, agreed work and price, non-refundable deposit before building, review before launch, launch checks and help with later changes. Make the pledge link part of the reassurance. Do not promise all future edits free or invent turnaround times.
4. Owners can edit their own pages and blog, or ask Kaizen to make changes. Explain the benefit without naming the technology. Keep the useful link to why Kaizen rebuilt its own website.
5. Confirm the company and where it works. Use the company name, number and registered office from `shared/legal.ts`. Label that address as the registered office, not somewhere customers can visit. Merseyside and West Yorkshire remain service areas; no new place-based claims or town pages.

## Design boundary

Use the released F typography, buttons, spacing and proof components. Create an independent About layout with labelled before/after figures and a concise process. Do not copy the homepage's bespoke comparison interaction or buyer-route pattern while Sean's review is pending. Its underlying F foundations and permissioned assets are available independently.

Remove the oversized name-definition section and repeated abstract promises. The meaning of Kaizen can stay as a short useful sentence. Keep one closing contact section and ordinary readable type sizes. No face placeholder or decorative substitute for proof. The first phone screen and the page as a whole should make sense before any JavaScript runs.
