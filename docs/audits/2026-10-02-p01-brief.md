## Audience and job

Owners and managing directors with a dated or slow website that brings in few calls. They know they need a better site, but may have paid for a disappointing one already. Some arrive by referral and need reassurance; others do not know Kaizen yet. The primary job is to start a free conversation about their site. The speed check remains the smaller next step.

This is the commercial web-design hub at `/`. Keep its existing title and description, which already put web design first and describe the offer honestly. Put web design in the H1. WordPress service intent belongs to `/services/wordpress-web-design/`; the tool and guides keep their existing owners. No local-SEO service pitch or new URL is needed. The full query-owner map remains S-01.

## Objection map and page shape

Structure: split hero; early reassurance; three buyer routes; real screenshot loading comparison; project steps; existing shared FAQ; four existing reviews; one closing ask. Fewer repeated arguments replace the old services/problem/about/why-us bands. Bright surfaces, Manifa headings and blue shared actions remain; one dark comparison band provides contrast. No carousel, faint scroll-reveal copy or purely decorative hero diamonds.
