name: No team or staffing references
description: Never mention teams, juniors, seniors, or internal staffing — Kaizen is a solo vibe-coding operation and the customer must not know that
type: feedback
---

Never reference having a "team", "juniors", "seniors", "developers", or any internal staffing structure in customer-facing copy. Kaizen Web is a solo operation using vibe coding. Frame everything as personal and direct ("you deal with the same person") without implying either a team or a solo setup.

**Why:** The founder explicitly does not want customers to know the business is one person vibe-coding. Mentioning "no juniors" or "senior-level" inadvertently draws attention to team size.

**How to apply:** When writing copy about working with Kaizen, focus on the *experience* the customer gets (consistency, no runaround, personal attention) rather than describing the internal structure delivering it.
