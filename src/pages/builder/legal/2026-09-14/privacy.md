## Information used by the Builder

- Account information: your email address, name, sign-in and security records, invitations and website membership. An owner may provide your email when inviting you.
- Work you supply: website source, page content, uploaded files, drafts, previews, saved changes and release history. This can include personal information that you or a website owner puts into a project.
- Service records: document-version acceptance, account and personal-data requests, owner responses, problem reports and operational records needed to investigate faults and protect the service.
- Technical information: requests to the service and its hosting providers can include IP address, browser details and access/security logs. Builder error reports use limited diagnostic details rather than copying the page's contents.

You need an email and sign-in details to use a hosted account. A name and valid email are required before saving changes to a connected website. Do not put passwords, private keys or unnecessary sensitive personal information into page content, diagnostic reports or privacy-request notes.

We use Supabase for hosted account, database and file services, GitHub for connected repositories and deployment workflows, Google Fonts for Builder typography, and server, network and email providers to deliver and protect the service. The current primary Builder database is configured in London. Hosting, provider support and network services can involve processing outside the UK. The final provider/location inventory and applicable transfer safeguards require Kaizen's review before public launch; contact our privacy address for the arrangements relevant to your project. We do not claim that all processing stays in the UK.

## Retention and backups

Account and website information is kept while needed to provide the service, comply with applicable obligations and resolve outstanding matters. Website source and release history can remain with the website after an editor leaves. Deleting an account is therefore separate from erasing particular personal information from a website or service record.

Builder diagnostic error records expire after 14 days. Closed personal-data request records are scheduled for deletion after two years; open requests remain until handled. Document acceptance history is kept while the account remains open and for up to two years after account deletion. An applicable legal hold or separate legal obligation may require a documented retention decision.

Operational backups have restricted access and separate retention. Erased data can remain in retained recovery points until those points expire or are replaced. It must be kept beyond ordinary use, and valid erasure decisions must be reapplied if a backup is restored. Backup rotation is not a promise of immediate removal from every retained snapshot. We will explain the relevant live-system and backup treatment when responding to an erasure request.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access or correct your personal information, request erasure, restrict or object to processing, or receive information in a portable form. Some rights have exceptions; for example, records may need to be kept to comply with law or defend a legal claim. A request should receive a response without undue delay, normally within one month; lawful clarification, identity checks or extensions may affect that period and must be explained.

Use Account to request a copy or erasure of your personal data and track the response, or email [privacy@kaizenweb.co.uk](mailto:privacy@kaizenweb.co.uk). You can use email without signing in or accepting new terms. We may need proportionate information to confirm identity and locate the records. A project request goes to the current website owner; an account request goes to Kaizen. Recording a request does not itself export data or delete a website. Any data copy must be delivered securely to the verified requester.

You can raise a complaint with us first. You also have the right to complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/). Where we rely on consent for any optional processing, you may withdraw that consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Optional tracking, if introduced, must be assessed and explained before it is enabled. Public websites and other Kaizen pages may have their own tracking choices and notices.

We keep a versioned copy of this notice and record which version you acknowledged. Changes will be explained when a new version is presented. The service does not use solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about individuals; technical access, publishing and usage controls protect the service and can be raised with Kaizen if you need a review.
